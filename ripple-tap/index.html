Ripple Tap

A calm one-finger river adventure

Tap the water to make ripples. Each ripple gently pushes your hero along the river: tap behind it to move forward, to the side to steer, right on top of it to slow down. Dodge rocks and whirlpools, pick up the little ones and bring the whole family home.

Four worlds in one game

  • Ripple Duck: a rubber duck and its ducklings through meadows, canal towns and jungle rivers.
  • Shell Voyage: a mother sea turtle leading her hatchlings across reefs, kelp forests and the glowing deep.
  • Waddle Wave: a penguin dad guiding his chicks through iceberg bays, glacier canyons and aurora nights.
  • Hoa Đăng: a floating lantern drifting down rivers on a lantern-festival night.

30 levels per world, an Endless mode, collectible characters and a daily gift. Every image and sound in the game is generated in code.
