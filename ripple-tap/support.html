Ripple Tap · Privacy policy

Questions, bugs or purchase issues? Email timeforwork789@icloud.com. Please include your device model, its iOS or Android version and what happened. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Frequently asked questions

I bought the VIP Pack. How do I get it back on a new device?

Open Shop or Settings and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy it. All characters in all four worlds unlock again.

Do I have to watch ads?

No. Ads only play when you tap a "watch an ad" button to double a reward, revive, or get free in-game currency. You can play the whole game without watching any.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

If your region requires consent for ads, open Settings and tap Ad privacy choices. On iPhone you can also turn tracking off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking; on Android, in Settings › Privacy › Ads.

How do I turn daily reminders on or off?

Use the Daily reminders switch in the game's Settings, or turn off notifications for Ripple Tap in your device settings.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device. Deleting the app removes your progress. Reset this edition in Settings clears the progress of the world you are playing. VIP purchases are kept by the store and can always be restored.
